Launch corridors, picked on purpose.

Apertra does not open everywhere at once. A corridor is only live when the structure behind it is in place: broker coverage, freight pickup, inspection partners and returns handling. At launch that means exporters in Canada and in six US states, shipping on seven lanes: Illinois, Michigan, Texas, Ohio, New York and Washington, the six states that import the most from Canada, roughly $185B a year between them. Buyers and importers can join from all of Canada and every US state from day one, because importing only needs the receiving side ready. The full mesh, five regions trading in every direction, is still the architecture this grows into. Corridors open as their structure lands, not before.

Businesses in states that are not export-enabled yet are not locked out. They join as buyers and importers from day one: build a catalogue, get products classified in advance, and request goods from exporters in Canada and the launch states. When their state switches on, exporting is a setting, not a new account.

Launch corridors, one set of rails

Every live corridor gets its own compliance pack (treaty, duty, labelling, and biosecurity where Australia is involved), but the workflow never changes: classify, price the landed cost, generate documents, book freight, fund escrow, release on clearance.

Live: Canada → Canada · Canada → US · Canada → UK · Canada → Netherlands · Canada → Australia · US → Canada · US → US.

Why states, honestly: nothing in US law stops an exporter in any state from shipping. What is missing in a state that is not live yet is Apertra's own structure there: freight pickup and consolidation coverage, inspection partners, and returns handling. Rather than sell a corridor that cannot be serviced, Apertra opens exporting state by state and lets everyone else import in the meantime. The first six are the top importers from Canada; Minnesota and Pennsylvania are next by volume. The same discipline applies between countries: each origin and destination pair gets its own broker arrangement and lane setup before it is sold.
