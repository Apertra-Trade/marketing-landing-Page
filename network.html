One network for buyers, exporters, and importers, in every region

Software alone does not create a trade. People on both sides of the border do. Apertra verifies every business once, wherever it is based, then connects the three sides of a shipment on the same rails. Matching works across the launch corridors: a buyer in any US state can be matched with an exporter in Canada or a US launch state, a Canadian buyer with a US exporter, in any combination the live lanes allow. As new corridors open, matching opens with them.

Exporters

You make it. We find who buys it.

Producers and brands selling abroad, across the US, Canada, Europe, Australia and New Zealand. Your catalogue, HS codes, and compliance readiness (including biosecurity readiness for AU/NZ) become your profile, visible only to matched buyers and importers.

  • Matched by product, corridor, and order size, not a generic directory listing
  • Quote with a real landed cost attached to the introduction
  • Your verification and on-time trade history travel with you
Importers

You bring it in. Suppliers arrive cleared.

Distributors and importers of record abroad. Instead of vetting foreign suppliers over email, you receive exporters whose goods are already classified and document-ready for your market.

  • See each exporter's compliance status for your country before you commit
  • Certificates of origin and labelling checks handled on the shared record
  • One onboarding covers every exporter you source from on Apertra
Buyers

You stock it. Payment stays protected.

Retailers, grocers, and wholesalers placing the order. You fund escrow once, track the shipment on the same timeline as the exporter, and release payment only when customs clears.

  • Free membership, always: buyers pay only inside a trade, never a monthly fee
  • Every seller identity and business verified before the first order
  • Escrow release tied to clearance, not to a promise
  • Reorder in one click from your trade history, no new paperwork
