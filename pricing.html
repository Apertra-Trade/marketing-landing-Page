Free to join. You pay when trade moves.

Apertra runs on a hybrid model, because a mesh only works if joining is easy and the demand side is never charged to walk in. Joining the Trade Network is free for everyone. Buyers and importers never pay membership. Exporters pay for the compliance engine once they trade seriously. And on every trade there is one embedded fee of 1.25%, with your membership credited against it, so you never pay twice for the same month.

On every trade: one fee of 1.25%, embedded

The transaction fee applies to every trade on the rails, whichever side you sit on, and it lives inside services you already buy. There is no separate platform tax on your invoice, so there is nothing visible for a buyer to route around.

Where the 1.25% lives

FX and escrow, combined0.75%
Freight and insurance margin~0.50%
Blended total, per trade1.25%

Banks charge 2.5% to 4% in hidden FX spread alone, and 1.5% to 3% for a letter of credit. Aggregated freight earns wholesale rates, so the customer still pays less than booking alone. Funds flow through regulated partners, so Apertra does not hold client money directly.

Your membership is credited against it

Each month, your membership fee is credited in full against that month's trade fees. Trade heavily and the membership effectively costs nothing extra. Trade lightly and the membership covers your fees, and the fixed cost of your verification, classification and audit vault.
