Why good products stay home

Businesses do not avoid exporting because demand is missing. They avoid it because four friction points make every order a gamble. Apertra removes each one inside the same workflow.

01

Classification and tariffs

Today

Guessing a 10-digit HS code leads to customs holds, seizures, or quietly overpaying duties for years.

With Apertra

Vision and language models read your specs, materials and photos, assign a defensible HS code, and flag free trade agreement exemptions automatically.

02

Regulation and labelling

Today

Missing an FDA rule, a CE mark, or a bilingual label requirement stops goods at the port, after you have paid freight.

With Apertra

A compliance gatekeeper checks each SKU against the destination rules and generates a pre-shipment checklist before anything leaves the warehouse.

03

Fragmented freight

Today

A broker, a forwarder, a drayage carrier and a warehouse, coordinated over email threads nobody can reconstruct.

With Apertra

Aggregated freight and brokerage through API partners, quoted and booked in one click, with one tracking timeline.

04

Payment and trust

Today

Ship and hope you get paid, or demand payment upfront and lose the buyer. Banks take 2.5% to 4% in hidden FX spread either way.

With Apertra

Escrow released on customs clearance, with FX and escrow combined at 0.75%, and trade finance offered inside the invoice flow.
